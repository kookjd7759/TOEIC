## 토익 필수 전치사 총정리  
1. at, on, in  
> ~에  
- [시간/시점]
    - at + 구체적인 시각 - at **6 A.M / 3 o'clock**
    - on + 몇월 몇일(날짜), ~day - on **April 7 / Saturday**
    - in + 월, 계절, 연도 - in **1999 / July / summer / the morning**
- [장소/위치]
    - at + 특정 지점 - at **the library / the station**
    - on + 접촉(면) - on **a table / a wall**
    - in + 3차원 공간, 행정구역 - in **Seoul / the calssroom**

2. before, after  
> before ~전에, after ~이후에  
- before + 시점 - before **Friday / 9 A.M**
- after + 시점/기간 - after **lunch / graduation / 3 P.M / three hours**

3. for, during  
> ~하는 동안  
- for + 숫자(기간) - for **three days / ten years**
- during + 특정 기간 명사 - during **the holiday / the meeting**

4. by, until  
> ~까지  
- by(일회성) - submit the report by **Friday / 7 P.M / tommorow**
- until(지속성) - Please wait until **tomorrow / midnight**

5. through, throughout  
> through ~을 관통하여, throughout ~내내(시간)/~도처에(장소)
- through - The train passes through the city
- throughout - She traveled througout the country / The song was popular throughout the year

6. between, among  
> ~사이에
- between (2) - between 2 and 4 P.M
- among (3이상) - among the emplyees/workers

7. with, within, without  
> with ~와 함께, within ~이내에, without ~없이
- with - with **her supervisor / the updated information**
- within - within **three days / walking distance**
- without - without **ID cards / warning**

8. because of, due to, owing to  
> ~이기 때문에
- because - because of bad weather
- due to - due to heavy traffic
- owing to - owing to safety concerns

9. ⭐ despite, in spite of  
> 비록 ~일지라도
- despite - despite the challenges
- in spite of - in spite of an in jury
