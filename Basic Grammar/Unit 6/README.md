## 등위접속사  
> 등위접속사 = 앞과 뒤가 동일 (단어-단어, 구-구, 절-절, + 품사도 동일해야 함)
> so 는 절만 연결
- and (그리고) - The team is hardworking and creative
- but (그러나) - We worked hard, but the deadline was too tight
- or (또는) - The meeting will be on Monday or Tuesday
- so (그래서/그러므로)- It was raining, so the event was canceled

## 등위상관접속  
> "both A and B"는 무조건 복수 취급, 나머지는 "B"에 수일치  
- Both A and B (A 와 B 둘 다) - Both Tom and his colleagues (has / have) submitted the reports
- Either A or B (A 또는 B 둘 중 하나) - Either the CFO or the board members (is / are) making the final decision
- Neither A nor B (A 도 아니고 B 도 아니다) - Neither the manager nor the employees (was / were) satisfied with the result
- not only A but also B (A 뿐만 아니라 B 도) - Not only the interns bur also the supervisor (is / was) attending the session

## 부사절 접속사 생김새  
> 부사절 접속사 + S + V는 부사절  
> 부사절은 종속절이므로 부사절, 주절 또는 주절 + 부사절 형태로 사용
- [example]
    - Even though she was tried, She went to work
    - She went to work even though She was tired  

#### 시간의 부사절 접속사  
- when : 할 때
- after : ~한 후에
- before : ~하기 전에
- until : ~할 때까지
- as soon as : ~하자마자
- [example]
    - When the manager arrives, we will start the meeting.
    - The lights truned on after the movie ended.

#### 조건의 부사절 접속사  
> **시간과 조건의 부사절에서는 "미래 내용"이라고 해도 "현재 시제"**로 사용  
> 과거일 경우에는 과거 시제 사용, 주절에서는 원래 시제 사용  
- if : 만약 ~라면
- unless : ~하지 않는다면
- [example] : If you register online, you will get a free gift

#### 양보의 부사절 접속사  
- although, though, even though : 비록 ~일지라도
- while : ~한 반면에
- [example]
    - Although it was raining, the concert continued
    - While sales increased in Asia, they declined in Europe

#### 이유의 부사절 접속사  
- because, since, as : ~하기 때문에
- [example]
    - The flight was delayed because it was snowing.
    - Since Mr.Smith was sick, he decided to take a day off.

#### 전치사 vs 부사절 접속사
| 의미 | 전치사 | 부사절 접속사 |
|:---:|:-----:|:-----------:|
| ~후에 | after | after |
| ~전에 | before | before |
| ~까지 | until | until |
| ~때문에 | because of, due to, owing to | because, since, as |
| ~일지라도 | despite, in spite of | although, though, even though |

#### 형용사절 접속사 (주격 관계대명사)  
> 명사 + 형용사절 접속사 + 주어(생략 가능) + 동사
> 위에서 명사는 선행사라고 함
- (선행사가 사람 or 사물) and (빈칸 뒤가 동사) : 주격 관계대명사가 정답 (사람이면 who 사물이면 which)  
- [example]
    - The employee (who / which) called you yesterday is from the sales team.
    - The computer (who / which) is on the desk belongs to Mr.Kim
