## 토익 필수 전치사 총정리  
1. at, on, in  
> ~에
    - [시간/시점]
        - at + 구체적인 시각 - at **6 A.M / 3 o'clock**
        - on + 몇월 몇일(날짜), ~day - on **April 7 / Saturday**
        - in + 월, 계절, 연도 - in **1999 / July / summer / the morning**
    - [장소/위치]
        - at + 특정 지점 - at the library / at the station
        - on + 접촉(면) - on a table / on a wall
        - in + 3차원 공간, 행정구역 - in Seoul / in the calssroom
2. before, after  
> before ~전에, after ~이후에
    - before + 시점 - before Friday / before 9 A.M
    - after + 시점/기간 - after lunch / after three hours
3. for, during  
> ~하는 동안
    - for + 숫자(기간) - for three days / for ten years
    - during + 특정 기간 명사 - during the holiday / during the meeting
4. by, until  
> ~까지
    - by(일회성) - submit the report by Friday/7 P.M
    - until(지속성) - Please wait until tomorrow
5. through, throughout
    - through - ~을 관통하여 - The train passes through the city
    - throughout - ~내내(시간), ~도처에(장) - The song was papular throughout the year
6. between, among
    - between - 둘 - between 2 and 4 P.M
    - among - 셋 이상 - among the emplyees/workers
    - 둘 다 '~사이에'라는 의미
7. with, within, without
    - with - ~와 함께 - with her supervisor / the updated information
    - within - ~이내에 - within three days / walking distance
    - without - ~없이 - without ID cards / warning
8. because of, due to, owing to
    - because of bad weather
    - due to heavy traffic
    - owing to safety concerns
    - 모두 '~이기 때문에'라는 의미
9. despite, in spite of 
    - despite the challenges
    - in spite of an in jury
    - 모두 '비록 ~일지라도'라는 의미
